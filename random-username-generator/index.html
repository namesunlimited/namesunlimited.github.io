    Naming advice

    What makes a good username?

    A good username is easy to read, say and type, and suits where you will use it. Beyond that, a few habits help:

    • Keep it readable. Avoid long number strings and look-alike characters that people will mistype.
    • Keep personal details out. If privacy matters, skip your birth year, hometown or full legal name.
    • Generate more than you need. The most obvious formats are taken first, so a backup list saves time.
    • Match the platform. A name that suits a professional network can feel stiff on a gaming site.
    By platform

    Which username style suits which platform?

    Social media usernames

    Social handles are read constantly in profile headers, comments and shared links, so clarity matters. A clean first.last or first_last pattern is easy to say aloud and easy to search for.

    Gaming usernames and gamertags

    Gaming handles can be more playful. A short word that hints at your playstyle or interests usually stands out more than a plain name with a number. The gamer style in the aesthetic username generator gives you handle ideas built from words rather than names.

    Professional and work usernames

    For a work profile, portfolio or company chat, a straightforward first.last format is usually the easiest for colleagues and recruiters to find.

    Anonymous or privacy-focused usernames

    If you do not want to be identified, avoid including your real name, birth year or location. A handle built from names unrelated to yours works well.

    Keep it lasting

    How do you build a username that lasts?

    Do not reuse the same username everywhere

    The same username on many sites makes it easier to link your accounts together, and if one site leaks its data, attackers can try that username elsewhere. Read why reusing a username is a security risk for the details.

    Pick a length you can live with

    Very short usernames are usually taken, and very long ones are awkward to type and easy to misspell. Somewhere in the middle, roughly 6 to 15 characters, is a practical range, but each platform sets its own limits.

    Add a meaningful word instead of random digits

    If your first choice is taken, adding a word that means something, such as a hobby or a role, looks more deliberate than a string of numbers.

    Check availability before you commit

    This tool cannot see what is registered on other sites. Search the exact handle on each platform you will use. More tips are in how to pick a username that is available.

    Common questions

    Frequently asked questions

    What makes a good username?

    A good username is easy to read, say and type, fits the platform, and does not expose details you want to keep private. Short, simple and consistent usually works best.

    How do you create a memorable username?

    Use real words instead of random characters, keep it short, and add one distinctive element such as a hobby, a place or a verb. Generate a large batch and pick the ones you can still remember after a day.

    Should a username include your real name?

    It helps if you want to be found, for example on professional profiles. For anonymous or privacy-focused accounts, choose a handle that does not include your real name, birth year or location.

    Will these usernames be available on every platform?

    The tool cannot check availability. Generate several options and test them on the platform where you want to register.

    What username formats does this generate?

    Four patterns: first and last name run together, first.last, first_last with a short number, and the.first with a number. Some results add an ending such as x, hq, tv or a number.

    Why do some usernames end with numbers?

    Many real accounts add numbers when their first choice is taken, so some formats include them. If you prefer cleaner handles, choose the ones without numbers.

    Can I use a generated username for a professional profile?

    Yes, the first.last style suits professional profiles. Check that it is available and consistent with your name or company.

    How many usernames can I generate at once?

    Up to 5000 per click. Because the name lists are limited, very large batches can contain repeated names, and smaller batches are easier to review.

    Can I download the list?
