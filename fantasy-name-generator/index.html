Phonetic Lore Guidelines for Fantasy Worldbuilders

A name grounds a character instantly into your fictional lore. Worldbuilders construct race-specific phonetic rules to convey history, physiology, and culture:

1. Elven Phonetics: Liquid Consonants and Flowing Vowels

Elven dialects prioritize fluid consonants (l, r, s, th) combined with soft diphthongs (ae, ia, el). Harsh stops are avoided, signifying elegance and ancient celestial ties.

2. Dwarven Dialects: Heavy Plosives and Harsh Consonants

Dwarven naming mirrors stonecraft and metallurgy: heavy plosives (b, d, g, k, t) combined with compound surnames describing deeds or ancestral gear (e.g., Stonehammer, Bronzebeard).
