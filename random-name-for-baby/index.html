    How does the random baby name generator work?

    It shows a shuffled batch of real first names, mixed boys and girls or filtered to one gender, so you can scan many baby name ideas quickly. Choose how many (up to 5000 per click), generate a batch, filter it, and download your shortlist as a CSV to share with a partner or family.

    The names come from built-in lists of 120 male and 120 female first names. Most are common in English-speaking countries, with some from other cultures. Batches of up to 200 avoid repeats, and larger ones can repeat names because the lists are finite.

    Browsing a shuffled list instead of an alphabetical one lets a name from the end of the alphabet get as much attention as one from the start.     Approaches

    How do you choose a baby name?

    Most parents combine a few approaches: collect names you like, test them with your surname and nicknames, and then consider meaning and family connections. A few checks help before you decide:

    • Say the full name aloud, including likely nicknames, to catch awkward combinations early.
    • Check the initials for unintended words.
    • Think about longevity. A name that feels trendy now may feel dated later, while classics tend to age more evenly.
    • Involve everyone who needs a say. Generate a batch together and note favourites separately before comparing.

    Naming by theme

    Some parents pick a theme first, such as nature (Willow, River, Hazel), the sky (Luna, Nova, Orion) or vintage revival (Arthur, Eleanor, Theodore), and choose within it. A theme narrows a long list quickly.

    Matching sibling names

    If you already have a child, look for a name that shares a style, sound or starting letter without being so close it causes confusion. Reading new options next to the sibling's name is a quick test.

    Family and heritage names

    Using a grandparent's name, or a family surname as a first or middle name, gives a name built-in meaning.

    Meaning-first naming

    Some parents start from a meaning, such as "hope", "light" or "protector", and look for names that carry it. The {L('/baby-name-generator/','baby name generator')} shows a short meaning and origin for each name.

    Popularity-aware naming

    Checking how common a name is, for example in the Social Security Administration's public baby name data, helps you tell a quiet classic from a name that three classmates will share. For how naming habits have changed, read the {L('/guides/baby-naming-trends/','baby naming trends guide')}.

    Collect names you like from several shuffled batches, then test each with your surname, nicknames and initials, and finally weigh meaning and family connections. A short list you revisit after a few days is easier to decide from than a long one.

    Yes. Use the gender filter to choose male, female or both.

    Can I see how a first and last name sound together?
