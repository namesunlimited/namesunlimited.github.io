    Naming advice

    How do you choose a girl name?

    Decide on a style first, then test each shortlisted name with your surname, its likely nicknames and its initials. Those checks remove most poor fits quickly.

    Start with a style, not a shortlist

    Decide whether you are drawn to soft, vintage, nature-inspired or bold modern names. Once the style feels right, individual names are easier to compare.

    Test the full name out loud

    Say the first name, middle name and surname together. What reads well on paper can sound clumsy aloud, especially if endings clash with your surname.

    Picture the nicknames

    Elizabeth becomes Liz, Eliza or Beth; Charlotte becomes Lottie or Charlie. If a nickname feels inevitable, make sure you like it.

    Check initials and monograms

    Write the first, middle and last initials together and make sure they do not spell something unfortunate.

    Weigh popularity against individuality

    A popular name means she will likely share it with classmates. A rare name stands out but may bring more spelling questions. There is no wrong answer, only a preference worth talking through.

    Look into the meaning and origin

    A name's background can turn a name you like into one you are sure about. Meanings are often approximate, so check more than one source.
