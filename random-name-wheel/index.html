What is a random name picker?

A random name picker, also called a random name selector or chooser, picks one entry from a list without any bias from you. Type or paste your names, one per line, and press Spin. The wheel turns and stops on a winner, with optional sound and a confetti effect. It works for names, numbers or any short text.

  1. Enter your names in the box, one per line, or load a preset (Classroom, Numbers 1 to 20, or Yes / No / Maybe).
  2. Press Spin Wheel and wait for the wheel to stop.
  3. Click Remove Winner to take the name off the list, or Spin Again to keep it in.
  4. Use Shuffle to reorder the list, and the Sound button to turn audio on or off.

How does the random name wheel work?

Each name gets an equal slice of the wheel. When you spin, the wheel starts at a randomly chosen speed and slows down until it stops, and the name under the pointer wins. The randomness comes from your browser's standard random function, so it suits classrooms, games and casual picks rather than regulated prize draws.

Your list is saved in your browser on your device so it survives a reload. It is not sent to a server.

How do you pick a random name without repeats?

Use Remove Winner after every spin. Each winner leaves the list, so the next spin can only land on someone new. When you want the whole order at once, press Shuffle and read the list from top to bottom instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the random name picker free?

Yes. It is free to use and does not need an account. You can create and spin as many lists as you like.

Is the wheel truly random?

Every name gets an equal slice, and the spin starts with a random speed that slows until a wedge stops under the pointer. It uses the browser’s standard random function, which is fine for classrooms, games and casual picks. For prize draws with legal requirements, use a method that meets your rules and keep a record of the result.

How do I pick names without repeats?

After each spin, click Remove Winner. The name is taken out of the list, so it cannot be picked again until you add it back.

Are my names saved?

The list is stored in your browser on your own device so it is still there after a reload. It is not sent to a server. Clear your browser data to remove it.

Can I mute the sound?

Yes. Click the Sound button to switch the audio on or off.

Why are some names shortened on the wheel?

Long names are shortened so they fit on a wedge. The winner panel shows the name from your list.
