How do the name generators work?

Each generator picks from built-in lists of names and word parts and combines them in your browser, following a simple pattern for that kind of name. The lists are short, so repeats are normal when you generate many batches. Treat the results as starting points: adjust a spelling, swap a surname, or combine ideas from two batches.

The baby, boy, girl and random baby tools use real first names. Fantasy and character tools use made-up or story-style names, and some entries may resemble names from books or games, so search any name you intend to publish.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a name generator?

A name generator produces names at random from lists, following a pattern for a purpose such as baby names, fantasy characters, usernames or test data. The results are ideas to start from, not definitive choices.

Which name generator should I use?

Use the baby, boy and girl generators for real first names, the character and fantasy generators for fiction, the username generators for handles, and the fake name generator for test data. The list above shows every tool by purpose.

Are the names real?

The baby, boy and girl generators use lists of real first names. Fantasy, elf and character generators combine invented or story-style names. Fake profiles are made up and are not real people.

Can I use the generated names in my own projects?

Generated names are combinations drawn from word lists, and you can use them in your own projects. A name that matches a famous character, brand or real organisation can still cause problems, so search any name you plan to publish. See the Terms of Service.

How do I spin custom names on the Random Name Wheel?

Open the random name wheel, paste your names into the box (one per line), and press Spin Wheel. Use Remove Winner to avoid repeats.

Can I export a list of names?

Yes. The baby, boy, girl, username, elf, city, school and band generators have Copy All and Download CSV. The fake name generator also exports JSON.

Are my names stored anywhere?

Names are generated in your browser. The wheel list and any saved fake-profile shortlist are kept in your browser on your own device. See the Privacy Policy for details.
