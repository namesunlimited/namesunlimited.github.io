    How does the random boy names generator work?

    It picks boy names at random from a built-in list of 120 male first names, most of them common in English-speaking countries and a few from other cultures, so the results are real names rather than invented ones. Choose first names only, last names only, or full names that pair a first name with a random surname, then set how many you want and press Generate.

    Each batch is shuffled and avoids repeats for batches of up to 200 names. Larger batches can repeat names because the list is finite. Use the filter box to narrow a batch (it matches letters anywhere in the name, not only at the start), copy the list, or download it as a CSV to share with a partner.

    Naming advice

    How do you choose a boy name?

    Decide on a style first, then test each shortlisted name with your surname, its likely nicknames and its initials. Those checks remove most poor fits quickly.

    Decide between classic and modern first

    Choosing between a traditional name and a contemporary one narrows the field faster than anything else.

    Say the full name out loud

    A first name that sounds good alone can clash with your surname. Read the whole name aloud and listen for awkward rhymes, repeated sounds or accidental phrases.

    Think about nicknames

    Names get shortened whether you plan for it or not: William becomes Will or Liam, Theodore becomes Theo or Teddy. Make sure you like the likely nickname.

    Check initials and monograms

    Put the first, middle and last initials together and make sure they do not spell something you would rather avoid.

    Decide how common you want the name to be

    A popular name means he will likely share it with classmates. A rare name makes him stand out but may bring more spelling questions. Neither is wrong.

    Look up the meaning

    Knowing a name's origin can make a good choice feel more personal, but check more than one source, since meanings are often approximate.
