    Name guide

    Dutch first names and family names

    Dutch given names range from short, familiar forms to names shared with neighbouring European languages. Family names often include particles such as de, van, van den or van der. These parts belong to the surname and should be kept with it when copying a generated result.

    Country naming notes

    Dutch name spelling and surname particles

    Dutch family names often include small connecting words called tussenvoegsels, such as van, de, van der and van den. These parts are meaningful elements of a surname, not decorative prefixes, and capitalisation can change depending on whether a surname is written with a person's initials or given name. For example, a surname may appear as “de Vries” in one context and “De Vries” in another. The generator keeps common particles inside the surname string so a result is not split into separate fields. Dutch given names also show regional, generational and international variation, and a short sample cannot represent every community in the country.

    Research notes and references

    Dutch Name Patterns, Surname Particles and Data Sources

    Dutch family names frequently include particles such as van, van der and de. These elements are part of the surname, not extra given names, so keep the full expression together when copying a result. The Dutch surname-bank entry for Jansen identifies it as a patronymic meaning ‘son of Jan’, illustrating how family names can preserve an older given name. Capitalisation can vary with the way a surname is written alongside a first name or initials; this generator uses a readable display form and does not attempt to reproduce every formal-register styling rule.

    The Netherlands also has a varied first-name landscape. The Sociale Verzekeringsbank (SVB) reported Noah and Noor as the leading boys’ and girls’ baby names in its 2025 list, with other names such as Liam, Luca, Olivia and Nora also appearing near the top. Regional preferences vary by province, and names used in the country come from Dutch, Frisian, biblical, international and other language traditions. A name used in the Netherlands is not necessarily Dutch in linguistic origin.

    Common questions

    Dutch name generator FAQs

    What is a Dutch name generator?

    It is a tool that combines Dutch given names and family names to suggest random name ideas for creative or sample-data use.

    Why do some Dutch surnames begin with van or de?

    Particles such as van, de and van der are common parts of Dutch family names. The tool preserves them as part of the surname.

    Can I generate Dutch male or female names?

    Yes. Select male, female or any gender, and choose first name, surname or full name output.

    Are all generated combinations common in the Netherlands?

    The lists contain names used in Dutch contexts, but a random combination is not a statement about how common a particular full name is.

    What does “van” mean in a Dutch surname?

    “Van” is a common Dutch surname particle that can mean “from” or “of” in a place-related surname. It is part of the family name and should normally be kept with the rest of the surname.

    Why is the first letter of a Dutch surname sometimes lowercase?

    Dutch surname particles may be capitalised differently depending on whether a first name or initials precede them. The generator stores a common written form, but formal records may follow specific styling rules.

    Are Dutch first names always Dutch in origin?

    No. Names used in the Netherlands come from Dutch, Frisian, biblical, international and other linguistic traditions. This generator is a sample bank, not a complete catalogue of origins.

    Does the generator provide official Dutch name statistics?

    No. It provides name ideas only. Consult a specialist name database or official population source for demographic research and verified frequency figures.

    Does a Dutch surname particle count as part of the surname?

    Yes. Keep forms such as van, van der and de with the rest of the family name when copying or sorting a full name.

    Are these the 100 most common Dutch surnames in exact rank order?

    No. The generator uses a curated surname bank informed by a published independent surname index. It is not a government-certified ranking or a live register extract.
