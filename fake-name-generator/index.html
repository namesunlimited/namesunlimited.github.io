What is a fake name generator?

A fake name generator creates sample people: a name plus matching details such as a username, email, address, phone number and job title. They are realistic enough to fill a form or a database, but they are not real individuals. Pick a gender and one of seven regions (United States, United Kingdom, India, Japan, Germany, Spain or France), choose a batch of 6, 12 or 24, and copy, save or export the results.

When should you use a fake name for testing?

Use a fake name whenever you need realistic placeholder data that is not tied to a real person. Common legitimate uses include:

  • Software testing and seed data. Fill registration flows, search indexes and pagination tests without using real customer records.
  • UI mockups and demos. Show a design or a product with believable names instead of "Lorem Ipsum" or "John Doe".
  • Sample forms and training. Give learners and reviewers example data they can enter safely.
  • Fiction and games. Create names for novels, scripts and non-player characters. For named characters, the character name generator offers genre options.

What does each generated profile include?

It works as a fake person generator for sample users and as a fake address generator for form tests, with the caveats below. Each profile has a full name, gender, country, city, street address, email address, username, phone number and job title. A few details about how the data is built help you use it correctly:

  • Names come from short regional lists, so results repeat if you generate many batches.
  • Addresses are a random street number, a street name and a city from the chosen region. They are not checked against real addresses.
  • Emails use sample domains such as mockmail.dev. Domains like these may exist in the real world, so do not use them for accounts you care about.
  • US phone numbers use a 555 prefix. Numbers for other countries follow a realistic format and could belong to a real person.
  • Job titles are mostly office and technology roles.

How do you export or save test data?

Use Copy All to paste a batch into a document, Export CSV for spreadsheets and database imports, or Export JSON for code and APIs. The shortlist feature lets you save favourite profiles across several batches and download them together. The shortlist is kept in your browser only.

What should you not use fake identities for?

Do not use generated data for fraud, impersonation, identity theft or to mislead people or services. The data is for testing, demonstrations and fiction. If a site or authority asks for accurate information, give it accurately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the generated people real?

No. Each profile is assembled at random from short lists of first names, surnames, streets, cities and job titles. Any match with a real person, address or number is a coincidence, so never treat the data as real.

Can I use fake names in software tests and mockups?

Yes. That is what the tool is for. Use the data in test databases, UI mockups, demos, screenshots and sample forms, and follow the Terms of Service.

Are the addresses, emails and phone numbers real?

No, but they can resemble real ones. Addresses combine a street and a city from lists. Emails use sample domains, and US numbers use a 555 prefix. Other countries use realistic formats, so do not send messages to, or call, generated contact details.

Can I use a fake name to sign up for a real account?

This tool is meant for testing, demos and fiction. Many services require accurate details, and giving false ones can break their rules. Do not use generated data to deceive or impersonate anyone.

How do I download several profiles at once?

Choose 6, 12 or 24 identities, click Generate Identities, then use Export CSV or Export JSON. You can also save profiles to a shortlist and download it.

Is my shortlist stored anywhere?

Saved profiles are kept in your browser on your own device and are not sent to a server. Clearing the shortlist or your browser data removes them.
