Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains what the site does and does not do with your information.

1. What the tools do with what you enter

The name generators run in your browser. Names you type into the Random Name Wheel, keywords you enter in the username tool and the profiles created by the fake name generator are processed on your own device. The site does not have a server that receives them, and we do not collect them.

2. Local storage on your device

Two tools save data in your browser's local storage so it is still there when you return: the Random Name Wheel remembers the entries in its list, and the Fake Name Generator remembers profiles you save to your shortlist. This data stays on your device. You can clear it with the Clear button in the fake name tool or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Hosting and third-party services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, GitHub may record technical request data such as IP addresses and browser details, under its own privacy statement. Pages that use the shared stylesheet also load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when those pages load.

Some pages load Google Tag Manager: the guides and the elf, city, school, band, boy names, girl names, random baby names and random username pages. Tag Manager can load analytics or other tags that are configured outside the site's code, so usage data or cookies may be collected on those pages. Names you generate are not intentionally passed to it. If you prefer not to be measured, you can block it in your browser or with a privacy extension, and the tools will still work.

The site does not currently include advertising scripts in its own code. If that changes, this policy will be updated.

4. The contact form

The form on the Contact page does not send what you type to us or store it. To contact us, use the public GitHub link on that page. Anything you post on GitHub is public and is handled under GitHub's terms.

5. Children and legal compliance

The site has no accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. This policy is a plain-language description of how the site works and is not legal advice. Privacy laws differ between countries, and we do not claim compliance with any particular regulation beyond what is described here.

6. Questions about privacy

If you have a question about this policy, use the link on the Contact page. Please do not post personal information in public.
