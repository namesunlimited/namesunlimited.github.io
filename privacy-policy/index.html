Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Names Unlimited ("we", "our", or "us") handles information, browser storage, cookies, and third-party services when you visit namesunlimited.github.io.

1. How Our Browser-Based Tools Process Data

All name generators and utility tools on Names Unlimited run directly in your web browser. Any names you type into the Random Name Wheel, keywords entered into the Username Generator, or mock profiles created with the Test Profile Generator are processed locally on your own device. We do not operate a backend server that collects or stores your generated names or inputs.

2. Local Storage on Your Device

Two tools optionally save data in your browser's localStorage so your entries remain available when you return:

  • Random Name Wheel: Remembers the entries in your custom wheel list between visits.
  • Test Profile Generator: Remembers mock profiles you save to your personal shortlist.

This data stays strictly on your device. You can clear it at any time using the Clear button inside the tool or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on Names Unlimited. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google advertising cookie and DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads to visitors based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • To learn more about how Google processes data when you use partner sites, please read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Hosting, Analytics & Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like standard web hosts, GitHub may record technical request data such as IP addresses and browser details for security and content delivery under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Pages also load typography from Google Fonts.

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic patterns, popular tools, and page performance. These tools may use cookies to collect anonymous usage data. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time, and our name generators will continue to work normally.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California (USA), you have rights regarding transparency, data access, deletion, and opting out of personalized advertising under regulations such as the GDPR and CCPA/CPRA. Because we do not store personal user profiles on our servers, you have full control to clear local browser data or opt out of personalized ad cookies using the links in Section 3.

6. Children's Privacy

Names Unlimited does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal details to us via email, please contact us so we can promptly delete the message.
