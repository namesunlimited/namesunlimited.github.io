Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how Names Unlimited ("we", "our", or "us") handles information, cookies, and third-party services when you visit namesunlimited.github.io.

1. How Our Browser-Based Tools Process Data

All name generators and utility tools on Names Unlimited execute directly within your web browser. Any names you enter into the Random Name Wheel, keywords typed into the Username Generator, or mock profiles created with the Test Profile Generator are processed locally on your device. We do not operate a backend database that stores or collects your generated names or tool inputs.

2. Local Storage on Your Device

To improve your experience, certain tools use your browser's native localStorage so your settings remain available when you return:

  • Random Name Wheel: Remembers custom name lists you enter so you do not have to retype them between sessions.
  • Test Profile Generator: Saves shortlisted mock profiles locally inside your browser until you clear them.

This data remains strictly on your own device and is never transmitted to us. You can erase this data at any time using the "Clear" button inside the tool or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on Names Unlimited. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the Google advertising cookie and DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized or non-personalized ads to our visitors based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • To learn more about how Google processes data when you use partner sites, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Hosting, Analytics & Third-Party Services

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like standard web hosting providers, GitHub may automatically log basic technical request metadata—such as IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and timestamps—for security, network integrity, and content delivery under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We also use Google Fonts for typography and Google Tag Manager / Google Analytics on our pages to understand aggregate traffic patterns, page views, and site performance. These services may set first-party or third-party cookies to measure anonymous usage statistics. You can disable cookies at any time through your individual browser settings without affecting the core functionality of our name generators.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your location, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California (USA), you have specific privacy rights under regulations such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to Access & Deletion: Since we do not store personal profiles on our servers, any tool data stored locally on your browser can be viewed and deleted directly by clearing your browser cache and local storage.
  • Right to Manage Consent & Opt-Out: Visitors from the EEA/UK can manage their advertising and cookie consent preferences via our site consent banner, and California residents may opt out of personalized cross-context behavioral advertising via the Google Ads Settings link above.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: Names Unlimited does not sell personal contact information to third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Names Unlimited does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has shared personal information with us via a support inquiry, please contact us immediately so we can remove the communication from our records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our tools, advertising partners, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date at the top.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out to us through our Contact Us page or email us directly at brandsfortheheart01@gmail.com.
