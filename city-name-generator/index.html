    About this list

    What is a city name generator?

    A city name generator creates fictional city, town and village names you can drop into a story, game or map. Press Generate to get a batch, filter it, copy it or download it as a CSV. The names are not real places, but they are built from the same pieces real place names use.

    How does this city name generator work?

    Real place names are usually made from a few recurring pieces: a prefix (North, Port, Lake, Fort), a root describing the landscape (-field, -brook, -ridge, -haven) and a settlement ending (-ton, -ville, -burg, -shire). The generator recombines 15 prefixes, 20 roots and 10 endings at random, and adds 10 ready-made standalone names such as Blackwater or Millbrook. Results read like plausible towns, but because the pieces are common, a result can match a real place by coincidence.

    Naming advice

    How do you build a believable fictional setting?

    Make names follow geography and history, keep one convention per region, and save distinctive names for the places that matter most.

    Let geography inform the name

    A coastal city, a mountain town and a river crossing carry different clues in real life (port, peak, ford). A name that matches the setting's geography makes the world feel considered.

    Keep one naming convention per region

    Places in the same region or culture should draw from similar prefixes and endings, just as many English towns end in -ton or -ham. When you need a place's school, try the school name generator.

    Save unique names for important locations

    A capital or main setting can have a distinctive one-of-a-kind name, while background towns can use simpler prefix and ending combinations.

    Say it out loud in dialogue

    Read your shortlist as if a character were saying "back home in ___". A name that is awkward to say will get in the way every time it appears.

    Search before you commit

    A quick search on your favourite result shows whether it is already a well-known real city, which could confuse readers. For fantasy peoples to live in your cities, see the fantasy name generator.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are these real city names?

    No. They are fictional names built from real naming patterns such as North, Port, -ton and -burg. Because the pieces are common, a result can coincide with a real place.

    What makes a good fictional city name?

    A good city name suits the setting's geography and culture, is easy to say in dialogue, and is distinct from other places on your map.

    Can I use this as a town name or village name generator?

    Yes. Short single-word results suit towns and villages, and prefix plus ending combinations suit larger cities.

    Do these work for fantasy settings?

    Yes. The pieces are fairly setting-neutral, so they suit modern, historical and fantasy worlds. Pick standalone names for a more invented feel.

    Can I use the names in a novel or game?

    Generated names are combinations of common words, but a name that matches a famous place or brand can still cause confusion. Search any name you plan to publish.

    Can I generate a whole map's worth of names?

    Yes. Generate a large batch (up to 5000) and download it as a CSV. Because the lists are limited, large batches may contain repeats or fewer names than requested.

    Do names repeat within a batch?

    Each batch skips duplicates, but the lists are finite, so very large requests may return fewer unique names.
