What does the baby name generator do?

It shows baby names together with their origin and a short meaning. Filter by boy, girl or unisex and by heritage (Latin, Greek, Hebrew, Norse, Sanskrit or Celtic), choose a batch of 12, 24 or 48, and click a name to copy it with its meaning.

How do you choose a baby name?

Start with names you genuinely like the sound of, then test each one against your surname, likely nicknames and initials, and only then weigh meaning and family connections. A simple order of steps keeps the list manageable:

  1. Collect. Generate a few batches and note every name that makes you pause.
  2. Say it with the surname. Read the full name aloud. Names often flow better when the first and last name differ in length or rhythm, though there is no fixed rule.
  3. Check the nicknames and initials. Consider what the name is likely to be shortened to and what the initials spell.
  4. Look at meaning and origin. Use these as one factor, and confirm anything that matters to you with more than one source.
  5. Live with it. Keep a short list for a few days before deciding.

How important is the meaning of a baby name?

Meaning matters as much as you want it to. Some parents choose a name for what it means, others for how it sounds or who it honours. A meaning can make a name feel personal, but it rarely decides how the name will be heard or used day to day.

How reliable are baby name meanings and origins?

Treat them as approximate. Many names have several proposed origins, and a short meaning is a simplification. Newer or modern-style names often have loosely attributed origins. The meanings in this tool are short, commonly cited summaries, so check a second source for any name you are seriously considering, and ask relatives about family traditions.

What are unisex names and gender-neutral baby names?

Unisex names, also called gender-neutral baby names, are names used for both boys and girls. Choose "Unisex / Gender-Neutral" in the filter to see examples such as Rowan, Asher, Kai, River, Avery, Sage, Quinn and Eden. How strongly a name is associated with one gender varies by country and changes over time, so check how it is used where you live.

How can you compare similar baby names?

Compare them side by side: write the full name for each option, say each one aloud, note the likely nickname, and look at spelling variants. If two names are close, the one that feels easier to say and spell for your family and community is often the better fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you choose a baby name?

Shortlist names you like the sound of, test them with your surname, nicknames and initials, then consider meaning and family significance. Giving yourself a few days with a short list usually makes the final choice clearer.

How important is the meaning of a baby name?
