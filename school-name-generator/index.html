    About this list

    What is a school name generator?

    A school name generator creates believable school names for stories, games, worldbuilding and projects. Choose a level (elementary, high school, or college and academy), set how many names you want, and press Generate.

    How does this school name generator work?

    Real schools are usually named after a person (often a local surname), a place or a neighbourhood, followed by a type such as "Elementary School", "High School" or "Academy". The generator combines 20 surname and place-style names, such as Lincoln, Riverside or Westbrook, with a suffix that fits the chosen level, so results read like real schools instead of "School #1". Because the pieces are common, a result may match a real school by coincidence.

    Naming advice

    How do you choose a believable school name?

    Match the name to the setting's history, keep the style consistent across a district, and choose a type that signals the kind of school you mean.

    Match the name to your setting's history

    Real schools are often named after a local figure, a founding family or a landmark. If your story has backstory for the town, a name that echoes it makes the setting feel lived-in.

    Keep naming consistent across a district

    Mixing "Lincoln Elementary" with "Starfall Academy" in one realistic town can feel like two settings colliding.

    Consider what the name signals

    "Academy", "Preparatory" and "Institute" read as private or selective; "Elementary", "High School" and "Middle School" read as standard public education.

    Say the full name in a sentence

    Try "she transferred to ___ in the fall" to check that it reads naturally in dialogue.

    Check it is not a famous real school

    A quick search on your favourite result helps avoid a name readers already associate with a real institution.

    Common questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Are these real school names?

    No. They are fictional names made from common place and surname styles plus standard school suffixes. A result can coincide with a real school, so search a name before you publish it.

    What makes a good school name?

    A good school name suits the school's setting, history and level, sounds natural in a sentence, and is distinct from famous real schools.

    Can I choose between elementary, high school and college styles?

    Yes. Use the School Level control to switch between Elementary, High School and College or Academy names.

    Can I use this for a high school or middle school name?

    The High School option covers high schools. There is no separate middle school option, but a result with the "Elementary" or "High School" style can be adapted by changing the suffix.

    Can I make a name for a fantasy or magic school?

    The generator is tuned for realistic names. For a fantasy feel, pair a place name from the city name generator with Academy or Institute.

    Can I use these names in a novel or game?

    Generated names are combinations of common words. A match with a well-known real school can still confuse readers, so search the name you choose.

    Do results repeat in the same batch?

    Each batch skips duplicates, but the lists are limited, so very large requests may return fewer unique names.
